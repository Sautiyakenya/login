What Counts as an Active Member

An Active Member of Sauti Ya Kanisa Kenya (SKK) could be defined more fully as one who:

  • Pays the required monthly subscription and other approved membership contributions faithfully.
  • Attends SKK physical meetings, conferences, summits, trainings, and county/regional gatherings.
  • Participates consistently in digital/online meetings and official communication platforms.
  • Recruits and introduces new churches, clergy, ministries, and individual members to SKK.
  • Supports SKK programmes, projects, and national initiatives.
  • Participates in county, regional, and national activities whenever called upon.
  • Promotes the vision, mission, values, and objectives of SKK.
  • Maintains unity, respect, integrity, and good Christian conduct within the organization.
  • Responds to official SKK communications, notices, meetings, and assignments.
  • Takes part in mobilization for SKK events and programmes.
  • Supports fellow members through fellowship, encouragement, prayer, and approved welfare initiatives.
  • Participates in committees, departments, ministries, or assignments according to gifts and appointment.
  • Encourages churches and ministers within their network to affiliate with SKK.
  • Protects the good name, reputation, property, and interests of SKK.
  • Observes SKK Constitution, policies, resolutions, leadership structures, and lawful directives.
  • Provides constructive ideas and recommendations for the growth of SKK.
  • Participates in leadership development, seminars, theological training, and capacity-building programmes where applicable.
  • Supports SKK's community outreach, advocacy, humanitarian, and clergy-empowerment initiatives.
  • Demonstrates commitment through service, not merely through registration or possession of a membership card.
  • Remains in good standing by fulfilling membership responsibilities consistently.
  • Has renewed his/her membership card.
